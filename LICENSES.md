## External Asset Research Notes

Kenney asset pages were reviewed as a commercial-safe benchmark because Kenney lists packs such as UI Pack and Isometric Tiles City under Creative Commons CC0. No third-party binary assets were imported from Kenney in this commit because direct shell downloads were blocked in the execution environment. The committed game assets are local project SVGs with clear commercial-use ownership.
